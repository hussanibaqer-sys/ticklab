TickLab v1.13.0 Drawing Tools Guide
======================================

CATEGORY SHORTCUTS
- Every left-rail category has two click areas.
- Click the large icon to activate the last tool used from that category.
- Click the small arrow to open the full category panel inside the chart.
- Search the folder, click a tool once, then place it on the chart.
- Drawing is one-shot by default. Enable Stay in Drawing Mode for repeated placement.
- Right-click or Escape cancels the current tool.

MEASURE
- The ruler button is directly above Magnet.
- Click the ruler and drag from A to B, or hold Shift and drag while a cursor tool is active.
- TickLab displays price difference, points, percentage, bars and elapsed time.
- Right-click or Escape clears it.

MAGNET
- Click Magnet to cycle Off, Weak and Strong.
- The button appears recessed/darker when active.
- With Magnet Off, hold Ctrl for temporary Strong magnet.
- With Magnet Weak/Strong, hold Ctrl to temporarily turn it off.
- The visible crosshair and active drawing anchor use the same snapped OHLC point.
- Existing anchors and the whole drawing do not jump.

FAVORITES
- Open a category and click the large star beside a tool.
- The horizontal Favorites bar opens automatically.
- Drag its left grip to move the bar.
- Drag tools left/right to reorder them.
- Right-click a favorite to remove it.
- The bar expands with additional tools and can be minimized or hidden.

QUICK EDITOR
- Single-click an existing drawing.
- A compact horizontal editor appears.
- Available controls adapt to the selected tool: line/fill/background color, pixels, style, opacity, fill, text, lock, templates, full settings and remove.
- Changes are visible immediately.

ADVANCED SETTINGS
- Double-click a drawing, right-click > Settings, or press the quick editor Settings button.
- Style, text, coordinates, visibility, levels, options and templates update as a live preview.
- Apply commits all changes as one undo step.
- Cancel restores the original drawing exactly.

FIBONACCI/GANN LEVELS
- Enable/disable, add, remove and reorder levels.
- Edit each value, label, color, pixel width, line style, price label and ratio/value label independently.
- Restore standard levels or save the complete setup as a template/default.

SELECTION
- Selected drawings show only compact anchor handles—no blue bounding rectangle.
- Drag an anchor to modify that point.
- Drag the body to move the entire unlocked drawing.
- Shift constrains compatible movement/angles.
