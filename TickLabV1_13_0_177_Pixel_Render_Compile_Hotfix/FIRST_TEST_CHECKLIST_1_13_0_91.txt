TickLab v1.13.0.91 — First test checklist

A. Fib Retracement (test first)
1. Open Settings.
2. Untick "Prices on levels". Price numbers must disappear immediately while ratios remain.
3. Retick prices, then untick "Level readings (0.5 / 0.618 / …)". Ratios must disappear while prices remain.
4. On one row, untick Price only. Only that level's price disappears.
5. On one row, untick Reading only. Only that level's ratio/reading disappears.
6. Untick On. Entire level disappears.
7. Change 0.618 to 0.700. Geometry and numeric reading should change live to 0.7/0.700 unless a custom text label is entered.
8. Add a level, remove it, move a level up/down, change its colour.

B. Background transparency
1. Click near 25%, 50%, 70%, 90% on Background transparent→solid slider.
2. It must move to approximately the clicked position, not jump only to 0 or 100%.
3. 0 must remove the background bands completely.
4. Maximum must make bands fully solid.

C. Other Fibonacci tools
- Repeat Price / Reading / On tests on Fib Channel, Trend Fib Extension, Time Zone, Trend Fib Time, Circles, Spiral, Speed Arcs, Speed Fan, Wedge and Pitchfan.

D. Gann
1. Gann Fan: readings must show 1/8, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 1/1 etc. when Reading is on.
2. Gann Fan: Price master/per-level Price checkboxes must visibly add/remove the price component.
3. Fixed Gann Square: drag the opposite corner diagonally. Whole square must grow/shrink while staying square/linked.
4. Gann Box: confirm internal diagonals/fan rays/arcs are visible and clicking them reselects the object.
5. Test per-level On / Price / Reading on Gann Box, both Gann Squares and Gann Fan.

E. Contrast
- Test light chart and dark chart.
- Readings should remain legible even when their level colour is very light or very dark.

If any single item fails, keep Folder 2 open and send the exact screenshot/video; do not move to Folder 3.
