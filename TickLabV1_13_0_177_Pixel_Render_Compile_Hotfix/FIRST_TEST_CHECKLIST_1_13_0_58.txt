TickLab v1.13.0.58 — First Test Checklist

BUILD
1. Close all TickLab processes and stop Visual Studio debugging.
2. Extract the ZIP to a normal local folder.
3. Run Clean-Restore-Build.cmd or Visual Studio 2022 Release Rebuild.

SPEED CONTROL
4. Open Replay.
5. Confirm the Speed selector is white with black text.
6. Open the Speed dropdown and confirm every speed option is readable black-on-white.

REPLAY START VIEWPORT
7. Scroll to historical candles and place the yellow replay line somewhere with visible empty/chart area available to its right.
8. Press Play once.
9. EXPECT: the yellow candle and future candles disappear immediately.
10. EXPECT: the remaining candles DO NOT jump/scroll to the far-right chart wall.
11. EXPECT: the same right-side area stays clear and replay grows into it as new candles form.

REVERSE / FORWARD
12. Let Replay run long enough to form part of a candle and preferably at least one next candle.
13. Press ◀ Reverse.
14. EXPECT: Replay runs backward tick-by-tick; candle close/high/low/body/wicks/volume retreat correctly.
15. EXPECT: Reverse stops at the yellow replay start and never crosses earlier than it.
16. Press Forward ▶.
17. EXPECT: the exact reversed raw ticks are replayed forward again, then normal unread ticks continue.
18. Change Speed and verify the selected speed affects both Reverse and Forward.
19. Pause with Play/Pause, then resume the selected direction.
20. Test Step tick and Step candle after reversing; they should advance forward from the current replay position.

REGRESSION
21. Test yellow/red Replay Range and confirm red auto-stop still works.
22. End Replay and confirm the continuously updated live chart returns immediately.
23. Confirm MT5 live capture continued while Replay was visible.
