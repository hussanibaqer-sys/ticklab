TickLab v1.13.0.52 — First Test Checklist
=========================================

PREPARE
1. Close every older TickLab process.
2. Extract this version outside OneDrive when possible.
3. Run Clean-Restore-Build.cmd or rebuild Release in Visual Studio 2022.
4. Open a symbol whose raw ticks have already been imported.

VERTICAL REPLAY LINE
1. Open Replay on Chart 1.
2. Press Set / drag replay line.
3. Drag directly on the yellow line and confirm it moves candle by candle.
4. Drag the chart from a place away from the yellow line.
   Expected: the chart pans normally and the line does not capture the drag.
5. Zoom with the mouse wheel.
   Expected: zoom continues normally.
6. Right-click the yellow line and select Remove replay line.
   Expected: the line disappears immediately.

RAW-TICK CANDLE FORMATION
1. Place the replay line on a candle covered by saved raw ticks.
2. Press Load ticks.
   Expected: future candles disappear and the first raw tick forms the selected candle immediately.
3. Press Step Tick several times.
   Expected: body, wick, close, spread and tick volume change one raw tick at a time.
4. Press Step Candle.
   Expected: exactly one candle completes; the next candle's first tick is not consumed.
5. Test Play/Pause at several speeds.
   Expected: candles grow continuously from saved tick timing.

CHART ISOLATION
1. Keep Chart 1 in replay and open Chart 2 live.
2. Click, pan and zoom Chart 2 repeatedly.
   Expected: Chart 1 remains in replay and Chart 2 continues live.
3. Attempt Refresh, Home, End or boundary paging on Chart 1.
   Expected: TickLab refuses to load future chart windows while replay is active.
4. Apply an indicator to Chart 1.
   Expected: it recalculates only from replay-visible candles.

RESTORE
1. Stop replay or right-click/remove the replay line during replay.
   Expected: the exact original chart history and viewport return.
2. Confirm live ticks resume on that chart.
3. Confirm no other chart, indicator, alert, drawing or Demo Trading state changed.
