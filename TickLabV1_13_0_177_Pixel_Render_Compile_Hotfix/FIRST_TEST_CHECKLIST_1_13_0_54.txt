TickLab v1.13.0.54 — First Test Checklist

BUILD
1. Close every TickLab window and stop Visual Studio debugging.
2. Extract the project outside OneDrive when possible.
3. Delete src\TickLab.App\bin and src\TickLab.App\obj.
4. Open TickLabV1_13_0_54.sln and run a Release Rebuild.

SINGLE REPLAY START
1. Move the chart to a past area so the latest/live candle is not the middle of the screen.
2. Open Replay.
3. Leave Replay range unticked.
4. Tick Replay line.
5. Confirm one yellow vertical line appears around the middle of the candles currently visible on screen, not on the latest/live candle.
6. Drag the yellow line to another candle. Confirm the chart does not reload and replay does not start.
7. Drag the chart away from the line and zoom/scroll. Confirm normal chart navigation still works.
8. Press Play. Confirm future candles disappear and replay begins from the first saved raw tick of the yellow-line candle.

REPLAY RANGE
1. End replay and restore the normal chart.
2. Tick Replay range.
3. Tick Replay line.
4. Confirm two vertical lines appear inside the currently visible candles: yellow start and red end.
5. Drag both independently. Confirm moving either line does not load or start replay.
6. Put the red line to the left of the yellow line and press Play. Confirm TickLab refuses the invalid range without changing chart data.
7. Put red to the right of yellow and press Play.
8. Confirm replay starts at the first raw tick of the yellow candle and stops automatically after the final raw tick of the red candle.
9. Confirm Step Tick and Step Candle never advance past the red end candle.

REMOVE / RESTORE
1. Untick Replay line before replay. Confirm both range lines disappear.
2. Tick it again, then right-click either selector and choose Remove replay line. Confirm both selectors are cleared and the checkbox is unticked.
3. Start replay, then End replay. Confirm the complete original chart and viewport are restored.
4. Confirm other charts remained live throughout replay.
