TickLab v1.13.0.50 — First Test Checklist
==========================================

A. Exact reported M1 scenario
1. Open the affected M1 chart and confirm saved history is available.
2. Press Refresh and note the candle count shown in TickLab.
3. Wait for multiple new ticks and at least one new M1 candle.
4. Confirm the candle count never falls to 1.
5. Confirm the chart never flashes "Connect to MT5 to load candle history".
6. Confirm the new live candle updates at the right edge while every older candle remains visible.

B. Multiple-chart regression
1. Open at least two charts for the same symbol, preferably with different timeframes.
2. Refresh one chart and continue clicking between charts while ticks arrive.
3. Confirm neither chart loses its history or adopts the other chart's candle list.
4. Maximize and restore one chart; confirm all charts retain their full loaded windows.

C. Previously fixed features
1. Confirm alerts, Demo Trading, history arrows, indicators and drawings behave exactly as in v1.13.0.49.
2. Confirm Code Editor, Demo Trading and Panel buttons still open only their own panels.
3. Confirm no MT5 bridge or saved-history file requires replacement.
