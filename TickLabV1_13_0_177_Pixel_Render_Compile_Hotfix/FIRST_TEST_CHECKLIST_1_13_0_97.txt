TickLab v1.13.0.97 — First Test Checklist

1. GANN BOX
- Draw Gann Box (not Gann Square).
- Compare it with your supplied TradingView top-left reference.
- Confirm the rectangle contains equal grid divisions, the complete fan/angle family, and nested colored quarter arcs.
- Confirm clicking a visible grid line, angle ray or arc reselects the Gann Box.

2. DO NOT REGRESS GANN SQUARES
- Draw Gann Square Fixed: confirm it still grows/shrinks diagonally exactly as in v1.13.0.96.
- Draw Gann Square: confirm its geometry is unchanged.

3. GANN FAN READINGS
- Draw Gann Fan.
- Turn Level readings ON and Prices on levels OFF.
- Confirm 1/8, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 1/1, 2/1, 3/1, 4/1, 8/1 appear on the rays.
- Turn Level readings OFF: ratios must disappear without changing ray geometry.
- Toggle per-level Reading boxes and confirm only that reading changes.

4. CHART RIGHT-CLICK MENU
- Right-click blank chart.
- Confirm the extra white strip/gutter on the left is gone.
- Open nested menus such as Indicators/Favorites Tabs and confirm submenu behavior still works.

5. EDIT / SETTINGS CONTRAST
- Open a drawing Edit/Settings tab in Light mode.
- Hover options such as Pixels/width/style entries: highlight and text must remain visibly different.
- Select an option and move the cursor away: selected text must remain readable.
- Repeat in Dark mode.
- Test quick-edit dropdowns/context menus as well.

If Visual Studio reports a compile error, send the exact error, filename and line number.
