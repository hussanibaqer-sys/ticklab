TickLab v1.13.0.111 — First Test Checklist

1. Arrow mark down
   - Open Geometry folder. Confirm Arrow mark down icon points DOWN.

2. Arrow Marker
   - Select Arrow Marker. Click tail once, tip once. It must finish after click #2.
   - Drag either endpoint: arrow must resize and rotate.
   - Click the filled arrow body/head: quick edit must open.

3. Path
   - Single-click 5-6 zig-zag points. Lines must be straight between anchors.
   - Double-click to finish. No duplicate endpoint/extra segment.
   - Confirm final endpoint has an arrowhead.
   - Click any segment or arrowhead: quick edit must open.

4. Arc
   - Place its 3 points. Confirm curved edge + straight closing chord + filled interior.
   - Change line color, background color, fill transparency and line pixels. Changes must update live.
   - Click curve, chord and fill: each must reselect the Arc.

5. Curve
   - Click endpoint A, click endpoint B. It must finish immediately after click #2.
   - Confirm one middle handle is auto-created. Drag it to reshape the curve.
   - Click any visible curve segment to reselect.

6. Double Curve
   - Click endpoint A, click endpoint B. It must finish immediately after click #2.
   - Confirm two middle control handles appear automatically.
   - Drag each middle control independently and confirm the curve changes without a doubled/parallel duplicate line.
   - Click anywhere on the visible curve to reselect.

7. Regression smoke test
   - Briefly open/use one already-working unrelated tool. No unrelated behavior should have changed.

Windows Visual Studio compile/runtime is the final gate.
