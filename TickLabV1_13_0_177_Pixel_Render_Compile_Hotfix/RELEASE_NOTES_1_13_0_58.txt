TickLab v1.13.0.58 — Replay Controls + Viewport Fix

Scope
-----
Replay controls and replay viewport behavior only. v1.13.0.57 replay identity fix and instant replay loading are preserved.

Changes
-------
1. Replay Speed selector readability
   - Speed selector uses a white background with black text.
   - Dropdown speed choices also use white background / black text.

2. Reverse / Forward replay buttons
   - Added ◀ Reverse and Forward ▶ buttons to the Replay window.
   - Reverse plays backward through raw ticks already revealed in the current replay.
   - Candle OHLC, wicks, tick volume, real volume and spread are restored tick-by-tick while reversing.
   - Forward reapplies the exact reversed ticks before continuing into unread raw ticks.
   - Reverse cannot move earlier than the yellow replay start.
   - Existing Play/Pause, Step tick and Step candle remain available.

3. Preserve clear right-side chart space when Replay starts
   - Starting Replay no longer pulls the remaining candles to the far-right chart wall after future candles are hidden.
   - TickLab anchors the completed candle immediately before the yellow replay start at its existing visual position.
   - The area that was to the right of the yellow line remains empty at Replay start and is naturally filled as replay candles form.
   - No forced Reset/fit or replay-start auto-scroll is performed.

Preserved
---------
- v1.13.0.57 replay identity/self-cancel fix.
- Instant raw-tick replay loading path.
- Hidden live MT5 state continues in the background during Replay.
- End Replay restores the continuously updated live chart.
- Replay Range yellow/red behavior and auto-stop.
- Trading, alerts, panels, indicators, drawings, history import/storage and MT5 bridge behavior are unchanged.
