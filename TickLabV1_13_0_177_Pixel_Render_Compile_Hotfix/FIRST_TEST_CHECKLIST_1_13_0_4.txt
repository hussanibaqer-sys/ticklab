TickLab v1.13.0.4 first test checklist

1. Extract to C:\TickLab\TickLabV1_13_0_4 outside OneDrive.
2. Open TickLabV1_13_0_4.sln, select Release, Clean Solution, then Rebuild Solution.
3. Open a drawing category, click the chart without selecting a tool, and confirm the category window closes.
4. Draw slow and fast curves with Pen and Highlighter; confirm they are smooth without broken zigzag segments.
5. Scroll the chart so blank future space is visible and draw far to the right of the newest candle.
6. Restart TickLab and confirm future-space drawings restore in the same positions.
7. Drag the right-workspace arrow handle fully left and right; confirm it follows the pointer and stops safely.
8. Click the handle without dragging; confirm collapse/restore still works.
9. Hold Shift and click point A, release Shift, then click point B.
10. Confirm the completed measurement remains until the next chart click, then disappears.
11. Open Icons, Stickers and Emoji; confirm the picker matches the dark TickLab theme.
12. Search and scroll each media folder; confirm coloured previews appear.
13. Place icons, stickers and faces on candles and in future chart space.
14. Confirm DRAWINGS, OBJECTS, MARKET, favourites, undo/redo, chart zoom and MT5 data remain functional.
