TickLab v1.13.0.52 change scope
================================

Approved scope: Replay Mode only.

Functional source changes:
- CandleChartControl.cs: replay-line hit testing, drawing-style drag capture and right-click removal.
- MarketReplayEngine.cs: expose the current replay candle bucket for exact Step Candle behavior.
- MainWindow.AlertsReplay.cs: bind replay to its owning chart, raw-tick stepping/playback, safe rendering, line removal and restore.
- MainWindow.ChartContexts.cs: route replay-line removal and block Refresh on the active replay chart.
- MainWindow.IndependentIndicators.cs: prevent replay indicators from reading future candles.
- MainWindow.xaml.cs: block older/newer/Home/End history injection into the replay chart.
- MarketReplayWindow.cs: clarify line interaction and removal instructions.

Release identity changes:
- TickLab.App.csproj
- MainWindow.xaml title
- VERSION.txt
- solution filename
- Clean-Restore-Build.cmd
- v1.13.0.52 validation and release documents

Explicitly outside scope:
- MT5 bridge code
- FileBridge/history storage formats
- Demo Trading logic
- alert engine
- right-side panel rail
- drawing engine other than temporary replay-line interaction
- symbol favourites
