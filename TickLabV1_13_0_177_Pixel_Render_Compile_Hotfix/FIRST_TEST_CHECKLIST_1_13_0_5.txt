TickLab v1.13.0.5 first test checklist

1. Extract to C:\TickLab\TickLabV1_13_0_5 outside OneDrive.
2. Open TickLabV1_13_0_5.sln in Visual Studio 2022.
3. Select Release, then Clean Solution and Rebuild Solution.
4. Confirm one compact top bar contains New Chart, workspace commands, symbol search and all timeframe buttons.
5. Confirm no duplicate custom minimize/maximize/close buttons exist for the main TickLab window.
6. Confirm the bottom dock is slim and contains chart/window tabs, Add Chart and MT5 status only—no timeframe buttons.
7. Confirm the connection indicator is at the far bottom-right.
8. Confirm the TL + TickLab wordmark appears in the lower-left chart area.
9. Click New Chart and confirm numbered chart 2 opens as a separate draggable Windows window.
10. Confirm chart 2 contains only its compact title strip, chart, right price scale and bottom time scale.
11. Confirm chart 2 has its own minimize, maximize/restore and close controls.
12. Drag chart 2 onto a second display, maximize it, restore it, minimize it and reactivate it from the bottom dock.
13. Create charts 3 and 4 and confirm numbering and bottom-dock selection remain correct.
14. Change symbol/timeframe on the main chart and confirm open price-chart windows refresh without freezing.
15. Open an indicator panel, detach it and confirm it appears in a numbered detached window and in the bottom selector.
16. Close a detached indicator/tool window and confirm the tool can be restored to the main chart workspace.
17. Drag Favorites toward every monitor edge and confirm it cannot disappear beyond the virtual desktop.
18. Hide Favorites with the Favorites button, press it again and confirm it restores visibly.
19. Drag the right workspace handle left and right and confirm its previous behaviour remains unchanged.
20. Confirm the drawing toolbar, adjacent tool folder, Pen/Highlighter smoothing, future-space drawing, measurement and coloured media still work.
21. Confirm Live Bridge V300, History Bridge V305 and Marker V109 remain unchanged and online.
