TickLab v1.13.0.41 — First Windows test checklist

Colour palette
[ ] Open Chart Settings > Chart background > colour picker.
[ ] Confirm Near Black is visible and selects exact shade #080808.
[ ] Hover Near Black for one second and confirm the tooltip says Near Black.

SL/TP controls
[ ] Open Demo Trading.
[ ] Select None, then each SL preset and each TP preset.
[ ] Confirm selected preset is dark blue with white text.
[ ] Confirm all inactive None/preset buttons are white with black text.
[ ] Check hover, pressed and disabled states for white-on-white text.

Pending orders
[ ] Open Pending Order.
[ ] Confirm Type and Expiration fields are white/light with black text.
[ ] Open both dropdowns and verify normal, hover, selected and disabled entries are readable.

Middle slide handle
[ ] Confirm the separate middle handle between Demo Trading and Code Editor is easy to click.
[ ] Click to open/close the right workspace.
[ ] Drag left/right to resize.
[ ] Confirm it does not freeze after releasing outside the handle.

Active trade levels
[ ] Open one Buy and one Sell demo position.
[ ] Confirm Entry label shows side, lot and running P/L only.
[ ] Confirm Entry, SL and TP exact prices are shown on the price scale.
[ ] Confirm SL/TP projected USD labels remain compact.
[ ] Drag SL and TP and confirm the existing behavior still works.

Trade history markings
[ ] Close a demo trade.
[ ] Confirm a line connects the entry point to the exit point.
[ ] Confirm entry and exit endpoint markers are visible.
[ ] Hover the line and verify direction, lot, entry/exit price/time, P/L, close reason and historical SL/TP.
[ ] Restart TickLab and confirm the path restores.
[ ] Delete selected/all history and confirm matching paths disappear.
