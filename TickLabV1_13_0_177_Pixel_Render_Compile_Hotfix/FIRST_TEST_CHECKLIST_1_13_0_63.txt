TickLab v1.13.0.63 — First Windows/live test checklist

BUILD
1. Close all TickLab processes and Visual Studio build processes.
2. Extract outside OneDrive when possible.
3. Run Clean-Restore-Build.cmd or rebuild TickLabV1_13_0_63.sln in Visual Studio 2022 Release mode.

ALERT CONTRAST
4. Open Alerts in both dark and light app themes. Confirm tabs, lists, checkboxes, buttons, text boxes and dropdown text are readable with no white-on-white areas.

RAW TICK CHART
5. Confirm Tick appears directly before 1s in the timeframe bar.
6. Select Tick. Confirm candles disappear and raw Bid/Ask tick lines appear.
7. Confirm the bottom time scale and right price scale are visible.
8. Wheel over the plot/time scale to zoom horizontally; wheel/drag the price scale to zoom vertically. Confirm you can zoom down to individual ticks.
9. Drag left. Confirm older saved ticks load without jumping the current viewport. Continue left to page through the available permanent raw-tick archive.
10. Press End / use the latest control and confirm the chart returns to live raw ticks.
11. Confirm multiple raw ticks sharing one millisecond retain their original sequence.
12. Open Chart Settings. Change Bid tick colour/pixels and Ask tick colour/pixels and confirm the Tick chart updates and persists.
13. Switch back to 1s and other candle timeframes. Confirm normal candles, Replay, indicators, alerts and history remain unchanged.
