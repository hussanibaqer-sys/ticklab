TickLab v1.13.0.24 first test

1. Select Chart Type > Candles.
2. Press End/Go Live so the latest candle and normal future space are visible.
3. Put the mouse at the left, middle and right of the plot and zoom in/out repeatedly.
4. Confirm the right/latest candle boundary never changes; only older candles enter/leave from the left.
5. Zoom past 400 visible candles and return to the default zoom.
6. Drag the bottom time scale left/right to zoom and confirm the right boundary remains fixed.
7. Scroll/drag through history, stop, then zoom. Confirm the current right boundary remains fixed at that historical position.
8. Confirm vertical price zoom still follows the mouse Y position.
9. Confirm candles remain crisp, uniformly spaced and centred.
