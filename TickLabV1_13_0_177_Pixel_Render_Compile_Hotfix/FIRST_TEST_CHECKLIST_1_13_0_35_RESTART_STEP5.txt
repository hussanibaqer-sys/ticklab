TickLab v1.13.0.35 Restart Step 5 — first Windows test

1. Clean bin and obj, then Rebuild Solution in Visual Studio 2022 with .NET 8.
2. Right-click the main price chart and a secondary price chart.
   - Confirm Add Indicator… is visible.
   - Confirm it opens the indicator library, not the applied list.
   - Apply an indicator and confirm it goes to the chart that was right-clicked.
3. Open the Demo Trade slide panel.
   - Confirm the slide button is vertically centred and does not overlap another control.
   - Confirm Balance, Equity, Floating P/L and Realized P/L are visible.
4. Open a demo Buy or Sell.
   - Confirm a live position card appears immediately.
   - Confirm Current and LIVE P/L update while price moves.
   - Confirm the position card × closes only that position.
5. On the source price chart, confirm Entry, SL and TP lines appear.
   - Entry must not drag.
   - Drag SL and TP vertically and release.
   - Confirm only that position updates in the panel and persistence survives restart.
   - Confirm an invalid SL/TP side is rejected and the line returns safely.
6. Click New Chart while no workspace partition is selected.
   - Confirm the floating chart header shows a visible ⠿ DRAG handle.
   - Drag it onto an empty workspace partition.
7. Confirm no real trade appears in MT5 and AutoTrading state is irrelevant to demo orders.
