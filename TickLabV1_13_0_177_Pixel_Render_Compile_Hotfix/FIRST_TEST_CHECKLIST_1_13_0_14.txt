TickLab v1.13.0.14 — first test checklist

BUILD
1. Close every TickLab and Visual Studio process.
2. Delete src\TickLab.App\bin and src\TickLab.App\obj.
3. Open TickLabV1_13_0_14.sln.
4. Clean Solution and Rebuild Solution.

ALERT BELL
1. Open a chart and right-click at a nearby price.
2. Choose Add Alert Here.
3. Confirm Sound and Desktop popup are checked.
4. Press Test bell. A clear bell should sound even if Windows Exclamation is disabled.
5. Save the alert.
6. Drag the horizontal alert line to a nearby price.
7. When price touches it, verify the bell repeats and the popup says the market touched that price.
8. Press OK. The bell must stop immediately and the popup must close.

SYNTHETIC CHARTS
1. Select a chart, then open Chart Type > Synthetic charts.
2. Test Heikin Ashi, Renko, Line Break, Kagi, Point & Figure and Range.
3. Open Synthetic chart settings and change one value.
4. Confirm only the selected chart changes.
5. Reopen TickLab and confirm chart type and settings are restored.
6. Switch back to Candles and confirm original MT5 candles are unchanged.
7. With an indicator applied, switch chart types and confirm the indicator refreshes.

REPLAY/ALERT REGRESSION
1. Start replay and then click End Replay. The vertical replay line must disappear.
2. Confirm draggable alert lines still remain after replay ends.
3. Confirm colour selection, floating-chart drag/attach controls and standard chart types still work.
