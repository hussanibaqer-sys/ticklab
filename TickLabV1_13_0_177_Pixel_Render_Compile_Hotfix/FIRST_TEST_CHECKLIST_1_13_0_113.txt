TickLab v1.13.0.113 — FIRST TEST CHECKLIST

1. TEXT
- Select Text, click once, edit the text.
- Confirm selected text has a clean box outline, not a detached anchor dot.
- Change text colour, font size and Anchor.
- Deselect/reselect by clicking directly on the letters.

2. NOTE
- Click anchor point, then click note location.
- Confirm straight leader + editable note box.
- Click leader and box separately; both must select the Note.

3. PRICE NOTE
- Click price anchor, then label location.
- Confirm formatted price only (no “Note”, no excessive raw decimals).
- Drag first point: displayed price changes. Drag second: only label position changes.

4. PIN
- One click should place map-pin + “Add text” box.
- Change pin/text styling and Anchor.
- Click pin body and text independently to reselect.

5. TABLE — TEST THIS CAREFULLY
- Create table with two placement points.
- Confirm blank 2×3 grid.
- Click inside a cell and type; caret/highlight should be visible.
- Backspace, Tab and Enter should work.
- Use +Column and +Row quick actions.
- Resize from all four corners.
- Click any cell/border to select the table.

6. CALLOUT
- Two clicks: anchor then bubble location.
- Confirm filled tapered tail + rounded editable bubble.
- Click tail and bubble to reselect.

7. COMMENT
- One click creates compact rounded comment bubble.
- Edit text/fill/font; click bubble/tail to reselect.

8. PRICE LABEL
- One click creates attached price bubble.
- Confirm price precision matches the chart symbol.
- Drag vertically and verify price updates.

9. SIGNPOST
- One click creates label with vertical post to the time axis.
- Click label or post to reselect.

10. FLAG
- One click creates small flag + pole.
- Click flag body or pole to reselect.

11. FREEZE CHECK
- Quickly open a known-good tool from an earlier folder (for example Path, Gann Box, Cyclic Lines) and confirm its prior behavior has not changed.
