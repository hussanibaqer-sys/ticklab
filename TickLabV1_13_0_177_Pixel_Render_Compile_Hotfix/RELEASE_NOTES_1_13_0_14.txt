TickLab v1.13.0.14

ALERT BELL REPAIR
- Replaced SystemSounds.Exclamation with a TickLab-generated PCM WAV bell.
- The bell no longer depends on the user's Windows sound scheme.
- Alert popup bell loops until OK is pressed or the popup is closed.
- Sound-only alerts play the same built-in bell once.
- Existing draggable horizontal alert lines and alert persistence are retained.

STEP 4 — SYNTHETIC CHARTS
- Heikin Ashi.
- Renko with configurable box size and reversal boxes.
- Line Break with configurable line count.
- Kagi with configurable reversal amount.
- Point & Figure with configurable box size and reversal boxes.
- Range chart with configurable range size.
- Synthetic settings are saved per chart and restored with the workspace.
- The original MT5 candle history remains untouched; synthetic bars are generated
  as an in-memory visual series whenever the selected chart type changes.
- Indicators use the currently displayed chart series, including synthetic bars.

DATA NOTE
- Heikin Ashi is calculated directly from loaded OHLC candles.
- Renko, Line Break, Kagi, Point & Figure, and Range are deterministically generated
  from the loaded candle closes. They do not invent real volume or overwrite source data.
