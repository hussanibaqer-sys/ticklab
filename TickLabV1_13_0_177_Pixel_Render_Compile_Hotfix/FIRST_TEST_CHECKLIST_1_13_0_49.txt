TickLab v1.13.0.49 — First Test Checklist
==========================================

A. Full history regression
1. Open the same symbol in two or more charts.
2. Confirm each chart shows its normal complete saved window, not only 30 minutes and not only the current candle.
3. Close/reopen TickLab with multiple saved chart panes.
4. Click a restored secondary chart that initially has no candle array in memory.
5. Confirm it loads the complete indexed local history for its own symbol/timeframe.
6. Leave charts running, maximize/restore one chart, and confirm all older candles remain present.
7. Confirm live candles continue at the right edge after the full history loads.

B. Alert text layout
1. Trigger a price alert with a normal title and message.
2. Confirm no letters are cut at the top or bottom.
3. Confirm the heading, title, message and OK button are fully visible.
4. Trigger a second alert while the first popup remains open.
5. Confirm the corner toast also shows complete text with proper top/bottom padding.
6. Confirm the bell still lasts at least five seconds.

C. Regression guard
1. Confirm Code Editor, Demo Trading and Panel handles still open only their own panels.
2. Confirm demo trading, history arrows, indicators, symbol favourites and alert-line removal behave exactly as in v1.13.0.48.
3. Confirm MT5 connection and imported history files are unchanged.
