TickLab v1.13.0.52 — Tick Replay Vertical Line Repair
=====================================================

REPLAY LINE
- The yellow replay-start line behaves like a drawing-style vertical line.
- Dragging begins only when the pointer is close to the line.
- Dragging elsewhere in the plot continues to pan the chart normally.
- Right-click the replay line and choose Remove replay line.
- Removing the line during active replay ends replay and restores the live chart.

RAW-TICK REPLAY
- Replay remains attached to the chart on which it was opened, even after another chart is clicked.
- The first saved raw tick is rendered immediately after loading.
- Play/Pause replays saved ticks according to their recorded millisecond timing and selected speed.
- Step Tick advances one raw tick.
- Step Candle processes the remainder of exactly one candle and stops before consuming the following candle's first tick.
- Candle open, high, low, close, spread, tick volume and available real volume are rebuilt from raw ticks.

REPLAY ISOLATION
- Only the selected replay chart is frozen from live updates.
- Other charts continue receiving normal live updates.
- Older/newer paging, Refresh, Home and End cannot inject future candles into the replay chart.
- Indicators on the replay chart calculate only from candles revealed by replay.
- Stopping replay restores the original candle lists, paging state and viewport.

PRESERVED
- v1.13.0.51 candle-list aliasing protection remains intact.
- Demo Trading, alerts, panel rail, drawings, symbol favourites, bridge discovery and saved-history formats were not redesigned.
- Protected MT5 bridge and FileBridge files remain unchanged.

BUILD NOTE
The generation environment has no Windows .NET/WPF compiler. Run Clean-Restore-Build.cmd on Windows with Visual Studio 2022 for the final compiler and visual test.
