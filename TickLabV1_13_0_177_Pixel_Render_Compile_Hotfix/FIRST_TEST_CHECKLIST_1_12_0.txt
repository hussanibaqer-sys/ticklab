TickLab v1.12.0 first test checklist

1. Extract to C:\TickLab\TickLabV1_12_0 (not OneDrive).
2. Open TickLabV1_12_0.sln.
3. Select Release and Rebuild Solution.
4. Launch at 100%, 125% and 150% Windows display scaling.
5. Confirm top command bar remains readable.
6. Add one drawing favorite and confirm it appears in the horizontal bar.
7. Drag two favorites left/right and restart TickLab.
8. Open each left-rail category flyout and confirm it stays inside the chart.
9. Draw several objects and verify they appear in the integrated Object Tree.
10. Test Object Tree selection, eye, lock and right-click actions.
11. Select a drawing and open compact editor/full settings.
12. Test Fibonacci level colour/fill/opacity live preview.
13. Test Measure and Magnet/Ctrl behavior.
14. Open marker controls and verify markers stay behind candles.
15. Leave TickLab open for at least 30 minutes and verify no lag/freeze.
