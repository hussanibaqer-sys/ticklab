TickLab v1.13.0.9 first test checklist

1. Extract to C:\TickLab\TickLabV1_13_0_9 outside OneDrive.
2. Open TickLabV1_13_0_9.sln in Visual Studio 2022.
3. Clean and rebuild Release | Any CPU.
4. Confirm all workspaces and partitions restore.
5. Double-click the price scale and time scale once; verify the correct scale resets and no chart disappears.
6. Open Chart Settings and test several colour swatches; verify the chart colour exactly matches the selected swatch.
7. Cancel Chart Settings; verify all preview colours reverse.
8. Test Dark and Light themes; chart colours must remain unchanged.
9. Hover and press toolbar, tab and window buttons; verify neutral hover and a subtle pressed impression.
10. Zoom out; verify the chart stops at 1,500 candles.
11. At normal density, verify approximately three pixels separate candle bodies.
12. Scroll and zoom multiple charts to confirm smooth rendering.
