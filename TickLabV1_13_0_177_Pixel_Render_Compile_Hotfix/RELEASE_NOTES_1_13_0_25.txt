TickLab v1.13.0.25 — Vertical-Only Zoom Isolation Hotfix

Scope: default Candles chart only.

Fixed:
- At the 1,500-bar horizontal zoom-out limit, further wheel-out input no longer executes any horizontal zoom, right-offset, clamp, viewport-publish, or horizontal redraw path.
- Horizontal and vertical wheel zoom are applied as one transaction with one viewport notification and one visual invalidation.
- The price-scale-only path preserves visible candle count, timeline range, newest-candle anchor, and right offset exactly.
- Compressed candle OHLC rows are clamped to drawable physical pixels, preventing zero-height disappearance at the plot-bottom boundary during deep vertical zoom-out.

No indicator, synthetic-chart, workspace, replay, alert, history, connector, or MQL5 protocol changes are included.
