TickLab v1.13.0.39 — Restart Step 7 Demo Trading Reliability and History

Purpose
Repair the Demo Trading panel and simulated scalp engine, add exact running-trade SL/TP controls, and preserve completed demo trades as chart history markings and statistics.

Changes in this release

Demo panel reliability and readability
- The thin vertical DEMO TRADING handle remains visible at the centre-right edge when the panel is closed.
- The handle opens/closes by click and slides continuously by left/right drag.
- Panel width is clamped to the current TickLab window so it cannot disappear fully beyond the right edge.
- Window resizing and lost mouse capture safely re-clamp/snap the panel.
- The demo/scalp panel uses a white/light surface with black text and readable hover/selected states.

Live simulated execution and profit/loss
- The demo engine reads the newest candle from the rendered chart before any copied display/history page.
- Market Buy opens at live Ask; Market Sell opens at live Bid.
- Buy positions are valued/closed and trigger SL/TP from live Bid.
- Sell positions are valued/closed and trigger SL/TP from live Ask.
- Position rows show entry, live Bid, live Ask, points moved, lot size, SL, TP and floating P/L.
- P/L uses symbol point size, tick size, per-lot tick value/contract size and selected volume.
- Positions refuse a reused pane whose symbol/timeframe no longer matches, preventing unrelated prices from being applied.
- Existing pending-order, persistence, account, reset and history behavior is retained.

Position closing and level modification
- Every open position has an individual × close control.
- Close Selected and Close All remain available.
- Right-clicking an active Entry, SL or TP chart line opens the exact position menu.
- SL and TP can be added or modified by exact price or points.
- SL and TP can be removed/cancelled.
- SL and TP lines remain draggable; releasing a line validates and saves only that exact position.
- Manual close uses Bid for Buy and Ask for Sell and records Manual as the close reason.

Persistent completed-trade chart markings
- Closed demo trades restore on the matching symbol/timeframe chart.
- Historical Entry, Exit, SL and TP markings show open/close time, direction, volume, realized P/L and close reason.
- Historical markings use distinct muted styles and do not expand the live chart auto-scale.
- A completed trade is drawn on one exact matching chart only, even if pane IDs are reused.

History deletion and statistics
- Delete selected history + markings removes one selected completed trade and its chart markings.
- Delete ALL demo history + chart markings requires confirmation and removes completed history only.
- Open positions and pending orders are not removed by history deletion.
- History summary shows total closed, profitable, losing and breakeven trades, winning total, losing total and net realized P/L.
- Statistics recalculate after deleting one or all completed trades.

Safety boundary
- Demo Trading remains strictly simulated.
- No real MT5 order API is called.
- MT5 bridge, FileBridge, history import and live-capture source files were not modified.

Validation boundary
Static source checks passed in the Linux environment. A real Windows Visual Studio 2022 / .NET 8 WPF clean build and runtime test are still required.
