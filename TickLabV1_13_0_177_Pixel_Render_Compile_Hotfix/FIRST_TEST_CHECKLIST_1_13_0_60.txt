TickLab v1.13.0.60 — first Replay test

1. Build and open a chart with saved raw ticks.
2. Enable Replay line: confirm START appears very small at the top and bottom of the yellow vertical line.
3. Enable Replay range: confirm END appears very small at the top and bottom of the red vertical line.
4. Press Play: confirm Replay collapses to the compact tab and continues normally.
5. Confirm compact tab contains End Replay and it restores the live chart.
6. Let replay advance several ticks, then press Reverse.
   EXPECT: the chart changes backward immediately on the click, then continues reversing.
7. Press Forward: confirm the reversed ticks replay forward again.
8. Confirm Speed still shows its selected value and Line/Range/Expand remain synchronized.
