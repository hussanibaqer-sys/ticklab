TickLab v1.13.0.17 — Uniform Candle Geometry Repair

CANDLE SPACING
- Restored the previous compact 3-pixel target gap.
- Adjacent candles now share one calculated slot boundary.
- Gap size no longer accumulates independent rounding errors across the chart.
- The same sequence is retained during horizontal and vertical zoom.

CRISP BODY BORDERS
- Removed WPF outline Pen rendering from candle bodies.
- Borders are drawn as filled physical-pixel strips inside the candle rectangle.
- Body top, bottom, left and right edges are snapped to the monitor's physical-pixel grid.
- WPF guideline sets remain active for the final device-aligned rectangles.

WICK CENTRING
- Wick X position is derived from the final body bounds, not from a separately rounded fractional slot centre.
- Wick and body therefore use the same centreline at normal zoom.
- The compressed renderer uses the same physical-pixel column for high/low and open/close.

ZOOM-OUT
- Removed the mixed fractional renderer that produced broken bodies and side-shifted wicks.
- Heavy zoom-out aggregates only candles sharing the same physical display column.
- Missing timeline gaps remain empty and are never aggregated across.
- The 1,500-visible-candle limit remains unchanged.

UNCHANGED
- Step 5 TPO, Session Volume Profile and Volume Footprint remain included.
- Alerts, replay, synthetic charts, workspaces and the MT5 bridge protocol are unchanged.
