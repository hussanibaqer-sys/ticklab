TickLab v1.13.0.42 first Windows test checklist

1. Clean bin and obj, restore, and rebuild the v1.13.0.42 solution.
2. Confirm the price chart is back at its previous width and is no longer shifted left.
3. Click PANEL: Drawings/Objects/Market opens. Click again: it closes.
4. Drag PANEL left/right and confirm resizing works.
5. Click CODE EDITOR: Code Editor opens. Click again: it closes.
6. Drag CODE EDITOR left/right and confirm resizing works.
7. Open Demo Trading > Order > Pending order Type. Check every popup entry is readable.
8. Open Expiration. Check Good till cancelled and Specified time are readable.
9. Check Order, Open positions, Pending orders and Trade history tab headers in selected/unselected/hover states.
10. Check open positions, pending orders and trade-history rows for readable selection colours.
11. Open Chart Settings, choose the chart background colour and confirm Default Chart Background matches the existing launch shade exactly.
12. Confirm Near Black remains a separate visibly different option.
13. Open and manage a demo trade to confirm existing demo trading behavior is unchanged.
