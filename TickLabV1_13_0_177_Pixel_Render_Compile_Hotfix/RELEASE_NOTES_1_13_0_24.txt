TickLab v1.13.0.24 — Basic Candle Newest-Anchor Zoom Hotfix

Scope
- Basic Candles chart only. No new indicator, chart-type, replay, alert or workspace features were added.

Root cause fixed
- v1.13.0.23 preserved a fixed negative right offset representing invisible future slots.
- At strong zoom-in levels, the visible-bar count became smaller than the future-space count, leaving zero real candles in the frame.
- Exact comparison with the default ten-slot future gap also incorrectly marked a zoomed live chart as historical, so newly appended candles could shift the viewport.

Changes
- Horizontal wheel zoom and time-scale drag now anchor the actual newest candle screen position.
- Future slots scale with the visible-bar count.
- The newest candle remains visible from 1 through 1,500 visible slots in a normal live view.
- The anchor ratio is retained through the one-bar tier, so zooming back out restores the newest candle position.
- Any viewport with the latest candle and only future space to its right remains in live-follow mode.
- Negative future-space offsets are preserved by viewport restore and settings sanitation.
- Historical views with a positive right offset retain their historical right boundary.

Unchanged
- Candle pixel geometry, borders, wicks and gaps.
- MT5 bridge protocol and MQL5 sources.
- Alerts, replay, indicators, workspaces and all existing chart types.
