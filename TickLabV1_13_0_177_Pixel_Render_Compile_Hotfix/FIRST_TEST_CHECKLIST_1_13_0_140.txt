TickLab v1.13.0.140 — First Runtime Test Checklist

1) FIND CANDLE BUSY STATE
- Open Find Candle.
- Search an old 1-second timestamp that takes long enough to observe UI state.
- Immediately confirm the button changes to “Finding…” with a rotating round-arrow.
- Confirm the Find button cannot be clicked again until the first search finishes.
- Rapid-double-click Find and confirm only one search starts.
- Confirm button returns to “Find candle” after success/failure.

2) PRESERVE v1.13.0.139 FIND CANDLE FIXES
- Search a 1-second candle about a month back.
- Confirm direct seek remains fast and does not sequentially generate/scan the whole month.
- Remove marker and confirm no thin residual marker remains.
- If any guide is intentionally left for fallback testing, confirm it can be selected and manually deleted.

3) TICK CHART — SCROLL MODE
- Switch the chart mouse-wheel toolbar control to Scroll mode.
- On Raw Tick Chart, wheel backward.
- Confirm the chart pans into older ticks without increasing the number of visible ticks.
- Continue to the oldest loaded edge and confirm older tick history loads while the viewport stays at the same horizontal density.
- Wheel forward toward live ticks and confirm normal forward panning.

4) TICK CHART — RIGHT-SIDE SPACE
- At live/latest ticks, confirm the normal right-side breathing room is visible.
- Scroll backward into history.
- Confirm the right-side blank space disappears and historical ticks use the full chart plot width.
- Return to live/End and confirm the right-side live space returns.

5) TICK CHART — 1600 LIMIT
- Switch to Zoom mode.
- Zoom horizontally out repeatedly over plot/time scale.
- Confirm horizontal zoom stops at 1,600 visible ticks and never exceeds it.
- Double-click/Fit Horizontal/Fit All and confirm they also never show more than 1,600 ticks.
- Confirm total stored history is still scrollable; only visible-frame density is limited.

6) TICK CHART — SMOOTH ZOOM
- In Zoom mode, wheel over the plot and verify combined horizontal/vertical zoom is smoother.
- Wheel over price scale and verify smooth vertical-only zoom.
- Wheel over time scale and verify smooth horizontal-only zoom.
- Drag price and time scales and verify reduced jumpiness.

7) REGRESSION
- Confirm normal candle chart remains unchanged.
- Confirm live raw ticks keep arriving while browsing history without snapping the viewport back to live.
- Confirm MT5 bridge/history/replay behavior remains normal.
