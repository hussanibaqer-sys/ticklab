TickLab v1.13.0.17 first test checklist

1. Open a standard Candles chart at default zoom.
2. Confirm every candle body has the same width.
3. Confirm every wick is centred inside its body.
4. Slowly zoom out through the detailed-to-compressed transition.
5. Confirm no candle becomes randomly thicker or thinner.
6. Confirm compressed candles remain one pixel wide with a visible one-pixel gap.
7. Zoom back in and confirm the sequence remains stable without horizontal flicker.
8. Resize the chart window and repeat the zoom test.
9. Verify drawings, crosshair, alerts and replay markers stay aligned with candle time positions.
10. Verify TPO, Session Volume Profile and Volume Footprint remain available.
