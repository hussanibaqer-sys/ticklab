TickLab v1.13.0.107 — first runtime test checklist

1. GHOST FEED CONSTRUCTION
   - Select Ghost Feed.
   - Single-click 5 or more turning points across the chart.
   - Confirm synthetic ghost candles appear live between each point.
   - Confirm the candles are NOT copies of the real chart candles underneath.
   - Double-click at the final point.
   - Confirm the drawing finishes without an extra duplicate final segment.

2. GHOST FEED SELECTION
   - Deselect the Ghost Feed.
   - Click a candle body in the middle of the feed: the whole object must select and quick edit must return.
   - Repeat on a wick far from the original construction points.
   - Drag the complete selected object and verify all segments move together.

3. BARS PATTERN CONSTRUCTION
   - Click source start, source end, then projection placement.
   - Confirm the projected blue line follows the selected historical bar-by-bar movement.
   - Confirm the source range/time chips appear while selected.

4. BARS PATTERN MOVE
   - Deselect and click the middle of the projected line.
   - Confirm selection/quick edit without finding an anchor.
   - Drag the visible projected pattern.
   - Confirm the copied shape stays identical and the historical source range does NOT move/change.

5. PROJECTION INTO FUTURE
   - Place a Bars Pattern close to/right of the latest candle.
   - Confirm the projected line can continue into future chart space rather than stopping at the last loaded candle.

6. FREEZE CHECK
   - Briefly open Long Position, Short Position and Position Forecast and confirm v1.13.0.106 behavior is unchanged.
   - Do not judge Step 3 volume/VWAP tools in this build; they are intentionally untouched.
