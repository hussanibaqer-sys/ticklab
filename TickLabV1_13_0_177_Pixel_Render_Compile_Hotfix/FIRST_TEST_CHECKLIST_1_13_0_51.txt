TickLab v1.13.0.51 — First Test Checklist

1. Build
- Run Clean-Restore-Build.cmd.
- Confirm the title shows v1.13.0.51.

2. Exact regression test
- Open an M1 chart with saved history.
- Press Refresh History and confirm the full chart appears.
- Wait for several new live ticks and at least one new M1 candle.
- Confirm the chart never collapses to one candle.
- Confirm “Connect to MT5 to load candle history” does not flash while the bridge is connected.

3. Multi-chart check
- Open two or more charts for the same symbol on different timeframes.
- Refresh one chart, then click between charts while ticks arrive.
- Confirm every chart preserves its history and continues updating.

4. Regression boundary
- Confirm alerts, Demo Trading, panel handles, indicators and drawings behave exactly as in v1.13.0.50.
