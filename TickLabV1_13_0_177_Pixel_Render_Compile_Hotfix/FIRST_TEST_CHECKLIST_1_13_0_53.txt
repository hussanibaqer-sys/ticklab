TickLab v1.13.0.53 — First Test Checklist

BUILD
1. Close every TickLab window and stop Visual Studio debugging.
2. Extract the project outside OneDrive when possible.
3. Delete src\TickLab.App\bin and src\TickLab.App\obj.
4. Open TickLabV1_13_0_53.sln and run a Release Rebuild.

REPLAY LINE PLACEMENT
1. Open a chart that has full candle history and saved raw ticks.
2. Open Replay. Confirm no yellow line is automatically placed on the newest candle.
3. Tick Replay line.
4. Click one exact past candle directly on the chart.
5. Confirm the yellow line appears on that candle, not the current candle.
6. Confirm the Replay date/time fields change to that candle's broker-server time.
7. Confirm TickLab begins loading ticks automatically; no separate Load ticks click should be necessary.
8. When loading completes, confirm Play becomes available and replay begins from the chosen past candle.

DRAG AND RELOAD
1. Pause replay.
2. Drag the yellow line to another visible past candle.
3. Release it and confirm date/time updates and ticks automatically reload for the new position.
4. Confirm dragging elsewhere on the chart pans normally and does not move the replay line.

REMOVE AND RESTORE
1. Untick Replay line while paused or running.
2. Confirm the line disappears and the complete normal chart and viewport are restored.
3. Tick it again, place it, then right-click the line and choose Remove replay line.
4. Confirm the checkbox becomes unticked.
5. Remove the line while ticks are still loading and confirm the stale load does not start replay afterward.

REPLAY ACCURACY
1. Press Play and confirm the candle grows tick by tick with body, wicks, spread and tick volume.
2. Confirm Step Tick advances one tick.
3. Confirm Step Candle completes exactly one candle.
4. Confirm other open charts remain live.
