TickLab v1.13.0.53 — Replay Line Checkbox and Auto-load Fix

CHANGED ONLY IN REPLAY MODE
- Replaced the Set / drag replay line button with a Replay line checkbox.
- Checking Replay line enters one-click placement mode; the user clicks the exact past candle where replay should begin.
- The replay line is not automatically placed on the newest candle.
- Releasing a newly placed or moved replay line updates the broker-server date/time fields and automatically loads saved raw ticks.
- Replay loading now retains the line's exact timestamp instead of falling back to an older typed time.
- The replay line remains visible and draggable after ticks are loaded.
- Unchecking Replay line removes it immediately. During an active replay it safely restores the complete pre-replay chart.
- Right-click Remove replay line also clears the checkbox.
- Removing or moving the line while ticks are loading cancels the stale load result.
- Normal chart panning remains available away from the replay line.

PRESERVED
- Tick-by-tick raw-tick candle construction from v1.13.0.52.
- Step Tick, Step Candle, Play/Pause, speed control and per-chart replay isolation.
- Full-history restoration after replay.
- Alerts, Demo Trading, panels, drawings, indicators, bridges and history formats were not intentionally changed.
