TickLab v1.13.0.6 first test checklist

1. Extract to C:\TickLab\TickLabV1_13_0_6 outside OneDrive.
2. Open TickLabV1_13_0_6.sln in Visual Studio 2022.
3. Select Release and Any CPU, then Clean Solution and Rebuild Solution.
4. On a clean settings profile, confirm Workspace 1 opens with Chart 1.
5. Open Charts 2, 3 and 4; close 1 and 3; confirm the next charts reuse IDs 1 then 3.
6. Click Divide and test Single, 2, 3, 4 and 6 partition layouts.
7. Confirm empty partitions show centred numbers with thin solid borders.
8. Double-click an empty partition and confirm a yellow selection frame appears.
9. Open a chart and confirm it attaches to the selected partition and clears the frame.
10. Drag a pane grip or floating chart number badge over an empty partition and confirm the magnetic yellow drop frame.
11. Drop it and confirm the pane fills only that partition.
12. Hover a pane and test partition maximize/restore, detach and close.
13. Test the docked workspace minimize, maximize and close controls; confirm maximize fills only the central TickLab workspace frame and close asks for confirmation.
14. Right-click a detached chart, choose Attach, and attach it to a numbered partition.
15. Right-click a detached chart, choose Make Workspace, and test each layout; confirm the chart occupies Partition 1.
16. Hover the top-left workspace hotspot and drag the whole workspace to a second monitor.
17. Maximize the detached workspace and confirm it fills that monitor.
18. Click Add Workspace several times and switch them from the bottom bar like pages.
19. Close a workspace containing items and test Cancel, Detach items and Close all.
20. Restart TickLab and confirm every workspace, layout, partition assignment and floating window returns.
21. While TickLab is running, simulate an abrupt termination, relaunch, and confirm the latest safely saved state or backup restores.
22. Disconnect the second monitor before relaunch and confirm detached windows return visibly on an available display.
23. Confirm the right price scale is thinner and chart width is increased.
24. Confirm the left rail has no duplicate TL logo, Favorites has no extra frame, and Brush opens Pen/Brush/Highlighter.
25. Draw quickly and slowly with Pen and Highlighter and confirm strokes are smooth and continuous.
26. Confirm Live Bridge V300, History Bridge V305 and Marker V109 remain unchanged and online.
