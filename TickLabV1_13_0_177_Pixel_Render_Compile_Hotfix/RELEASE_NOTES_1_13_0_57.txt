TickLab v1.13.0.57 — Replay Identity Fix

Scope
-----
Replay-only hotfix on top of v1.13.0.56.

Root cause fixed
----------------
Replay captured ChartRuntimeContext.IdentityGeneration before calling
ResetContextHistoryPaging(context). That reset increments IdentityGeneration by design.
After the asynchronous raw-tick read completed, Replay compared the old generation with
the incremented generation and treated its own reset as a user chart change. Result:
Replay cancelled itself every time with the target-changed/failure path.

Correction
----------
The replay identity guard is now captured immediately AFTER ResetContextHistoryPaging.
Real symbol/timeframe/chart changes during the asynchronous read are still rejected, but
Replay no longer rejects its own setup.

Preserved behavior
------------------
- Play hides the yellow candle and future candles before tick I/O.
- Live MT5 processing continues in hidden live state.
- Fast raw tick path and permanent binary archive fallback remain unchanged.
- Replay Range, selectors, trading, alerts, panels, indicators, history and bridge files
  are otherwise unchanged.
