TickLab v1.13.0.155 — Synthetic Seconds Lock

BASE
- Rebuilt directly from v1.13.0.152 — Tick Render + Drawing Smoothness.
- v1.13.0.153 and v1.13.0.154 changes are NOT included.

ONLY FUNCTIONAL CHANGE
- Synthetic chart types are locked on every seconds timeframe (built-in or custom).
- Locked types: Heikin Ashi, Renko, Line Break, Kagi, Point & Figure, Range.
- While a seconds timeframe is active, the Synthetic charts menu/settings are disabled.
- Direct/programmatic synthetic selection on seconds is rejected.
- Switching from a synthetic minute-or-higher chart to a seconds timeframe automatically uses normal Candles.
- Saved workspaces containing synthetic + seconds are normalized to Candles when restored.
- Synthetic chart types remain available normally on 1-minute and higher timeframes.

PROTECTED
- No v153/v154 synthetic paging/history modifications are present.
- v152 Tick renderer and drawing smoothness changes are preserved.
- Seconds history/paging/aggregation remains exactly v152.
- Tick history, Tick Find, Candle Finder, Replay, watchdog/integrity agents, FileBridge/MT5/MQL, drawings, indicators and other chart types are unchanged except for the synthetic-seconds selection guard described above.
