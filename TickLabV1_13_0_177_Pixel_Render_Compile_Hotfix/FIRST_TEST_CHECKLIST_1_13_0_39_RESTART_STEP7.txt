TickLab v1.13.0.39 — First runtime test checklist

A. Demo panel handle
1. Start TickLab with the demo panel closed.
2. Confirm the thin vertical DEMO TRADING handle remains visible at centre-right.
3. Click the handle to open, then click again to close.
4. Drag left to open and right to close.
5. Resize the main window smaller and larger; confirm the handle never disappears off-screen.
6. Confirm the handle does not block unrelated chart buttons.

B. Readability
1. Open every Demo Trading tab.
2. Confirm labels, input values, preset buttons, open positions, pending orders and history are black/readable on white/light backgrounds.
3. Confirm hover and selected preset states remain visible.

C. Market execution and live P/L
1. Select a live chart and note its displayed Bid and Ask.
2. With No SL and No TP selected, open a Buy; confirm entry equals Ask.
3. Open a Sell; confirm entry equals Bid.
4. Confirm each position immediately shows live Bid, Ask, points moved, lot and floating P/L.
5. Let price move; confirm Buy P/L follows Bid and Sell P/L follows Ask continuously.
6. Test 0.01, 0.02 and another lot size; confirm P/L scales proportionally.
7. Change a pane to another symbol; confirm an existing trade never uses the new unrelated symbol price.

D. Close controls
1. Close one position using its × button.
2. Test Close selected now.
3. Test Close all now.
4. Confirm balance, equity, realized P/L and history update immediately.
5. Confirm Buy closes at Bid and Sell closes at Ask.

E. SL/TP modification
1. Open a trade with no SL/TP.
2. Right-click its Entry line and add SL by exact price, then TP by points.
3. Right-click the SL line and modify by exact price and by points.
4. Right-click the TP line and modify by exact price and by points.
5. Remove/cancel SL and TP from their context menus.
6. Add levels again and drag both lines; confirm only the exact position changes.
7. Confirm invalid-direction levels are rejected.
8. Let a Buy SL/TP trigger from Bid and a Sell SL/TP trigger from Ask.

F. Completed trade markings
1. Close trades manually and through SL/TP.
2. Confirm distinct historical Entry, Exit, SL and TP markings remain on the correct chart.
3. Confirm labels show direction, lot, open/close time, realized P/L and close reason.
4. Restart TickLab and confirm history markings restore.
5. Confirm historical markings do not stretch the chart price scale.

G. History deletion and statistics
1. Confirm totals for Closed, Profit trades, Loss trades, Breakeven, Winning total, Losing total and Net P/L.
2. Select one history trade and delete it; confirm only its record/markings disappear and totals recalculate.
3. Use Delete ALL demo history + chart markings and confirm the warning.
4. Confirm completed history/markings clear while open positions and pending orders remain.

H. Regression smoke test
1. Confirm indicator right-click menus from v1.13.0.38 still work.
2. Confirm chart colour settings and Bid/Ask lines still work.
3. Confirm MT5 connection, history import and live chart capture remain unchanged.
4. Confirm Demo Trading never sends a real MT5 order.
