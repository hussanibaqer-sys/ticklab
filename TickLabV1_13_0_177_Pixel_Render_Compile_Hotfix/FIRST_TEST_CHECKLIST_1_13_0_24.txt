TickLab v1.13.0.24 first test — basic Candles only

1. Open a normal Candles chart at the latest market position.
2. Place the pointer anywhere inside the plot and zoom in repeatedly until only 1–5 bars fit.
   Expected: at least the newest candle always remains visible.
3. Zoom back out to 100, 400 and 1,500 visible slots.
   Expected: the newest candle remains at the same approximate right-side position; it does not disappear and return.
4. Repeat zoom in/out at least 20 times.
   Expected: no cumulative left/right drift.
5. Drag the bottom time scale in both directions.
   Expected: same behavior as wheel zoom; no empty chart caused by future space.
6. Leave the chart zoomed and wait for a new candle to open.
   Expected: the viewport remains live and does not jump.
7. Close and reopen TickLab.
   Expected: saved future space is restored instead of being forced to zero.

Do not evaluate other chart types in this hotfix. They will be audited only after the basic Candles chart is confirmed stable.
