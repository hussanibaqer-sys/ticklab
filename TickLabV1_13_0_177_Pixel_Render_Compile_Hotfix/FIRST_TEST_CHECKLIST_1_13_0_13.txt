TickLab v1.13.0.13 first test checklist

1. Open a chart and right-click a price. Add and save the alert.
2. Confirm a yellow/orange horizontal bell line appears.
3. Drag the alert line vertically and release. Reopen Alert Manager and confirm the threshold changed.
4. Move live price through the alert. Confirm the repeating bell and “Market touched that price” popup. Press OK and confirm the sound stops.
5. Open Replay, place the vertical replay line, then click End replay before loading ticks. Confirm the line is removed.
6. Repeat after loading/playing replay. Confirm End replay restores the live chart and removes the vertical line.
7. Select each chart type from the top Chart Type button and verify only the selected chart changes.
8. Restart TickLab and confirm every chart restores its own chart type.
9. Verify workspace partitions, detached windows, colours, templates and drawing synchronization still work.
